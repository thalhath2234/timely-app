User report 2026-10-31 (desktop, Trip to Tokyo doc): "Make task" margin offers (from #255) are not level with their rows when the Outline card is expanded; folding and unfolding breaks them again. User asked for design options first ("design some options"), then build the pick. Said "use ultracode" (workflows) and "use the claude haiku 5.5 for testing" for this thread.

Two causes (proven in the cloud repro):
1. By design: MarginHints.place() (docHints.tsx) floors every card at the bottom of the summary card, so rows beside the Outline/summary cards get their offer pushed far below the row.
2. Bug: findLines() caches row elements; the editor re-creates checklist <ul>/<li> DOM without an "update" event (seen right at the Outline chevron click), so place() skips those cards and they keep their old offset, piling onto other rows. Fix on branch fix/make-task-offer-alignment (resolve the row from its doc position at place time; hide a card with no row).

Phone is not affected the same way: it draws a marker at the row end inside the editor (editorHtml.ts LineHints).
